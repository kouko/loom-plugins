# Relative iteration 7 — repeated candidate behavior

## Outcome

This round repeated round 6's identical candidate overlay. The incomplete CSV
case again acknowledged a required unanswered product question, labeled it
weak and asked for confirmation; it also introduced a new current-page versus
all-pages question and asked for a yes in the same reply. Its urgency answer
was also absent from the case. This repeats the round 4 and released-baseline
premature-confirmation failure despite the aligned template; rounds 5 and 6
did ask for the missing facts. The identical overlay therefore did not produce
stable behavior in these samples.

Both the complete CSV table and stock report table invented current-state
selection details; the CSV table also claimed there was no fixed format.
The round 6 comparison wording did not prevent the same type of failure in
this repeat. The requested before/after flow had labels but omitted the two
outer frames. The explicit ongoing hold and the unambiguous automatic
publication disclosure were both retained. The ready engineering cases
created open intent drafts before confirmation. The unknown-date answer
asserted that no other date evidence existed based on the lack of repository
commits; that does not rule out a record elsewhere.

The case wrappers saying `Do not publish` without specifying duration remain
ambiguous, so their treatment is not counted as a definite publication-consent
failure. No observation proves that a particular line caused the differences.
Repeated case reuse and model variance limit inference; adding another generic
grounding instruction has not earned evidence of a reliable benefit.
