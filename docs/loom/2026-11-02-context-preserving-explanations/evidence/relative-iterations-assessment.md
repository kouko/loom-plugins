# Relative iteration assessment

## Decision

Keep the smaller candidate as a working draft, without publication or merge.
The observations support improvement in specific situations, especially not
offering a guessed replacement for an unknown date and spelling out the
automatic-publication opt-out through the time of publication. They do not
establish that the full change is better overall or ready to release: the
candidate still invents current-state facts, sometimes confirms with required
facts missing, and does not consistently follow the requested graph style.
Do not require perfect model accuracy; do require a defensible comparison and
the existing independent review and acceptance steps.

The round 6 comparison-specific addition was removed after round 7 failed to
show a stable benefit. The retained candidate overlay equals rounds 5 and 8.
Its product intent templates no longer call required product value information
optional, but rounds 4 and 7 show that explicit instructions alone did not
ensure compliance. This is not evidence that the edits caused the errors.

## Evidence

- The initial and round 9 released-baseline observations both prematurely
  requested confirmation for the incomplete CSV. Candidate rounds 4 and 7
  also did so; the other six candidate rounds asked for missing information
  first. These are repeated observations of one case, not population rates.
- The repeated baseline unknown-date case proposed a guessed-date option;
  candidate runs did not offer that option, although some made unsupported
  claims about available evidence. The ready default-publication candidate
  samples disclosed later opt-out, while the round 9 baseline phrased opt-out
  as something to do when answering.
- The stock PDF output repeatedly invented details about the present manual
  report. Candidate tables and the requested diagram introduced their own
  unsupported or overbroad statements; frame and alignment behavior varied.
  The baseline also produced unsupported or contradictory claims. No
  comprehensive, blinded severity ranking was performed.
- Each completed round used one first-party Claude Sonnet session per case;
  the eight synthetic cases were repeatedly reused and baseline and candidate
  runs were not randomly interleaved. Results are not generalizable error
  rates and provide no evidence for other models or hosts.
- Round 10 was interrupted by Anthropic's five-hour limit after three failed
  calls; its other five did not start. The sum of CLI estimated costs for
  rounds 1–10, including these three incomplete calls, is USD 10.5920193;
  this excludes the prior original paired comparison and earlier work.
- The full offline locked package suite passed on the final runtime overlay,
  which matches round 5. This is mechanical verification, not behavioral or
  independent acceptance evidence. No final review, publication or merge has
  been performed for the present uncommitted tree.
