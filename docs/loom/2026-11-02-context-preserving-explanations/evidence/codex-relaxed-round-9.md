# Relaxed comparison — round 9

A new ready customer-service return-export fixture compared the released
baseline and the updated candidate from round 8. Both described the affected
staff, manual copying, ordered fields, filtered and permission-limited data,
non-mutation, exclusions and deadline. The baseline additionally said the CSV
fields were *only* the listed ones; the request supplied their order but did
not explicitly exclude further fields. Candidate did not add that exclusion.
Both disclosed review and publication checks, non-forced push, PR, opt-out
before publication and separate merge. Both included internal scope-check
bookkeeping; candidate also discussed fixture code and maintenance cost.

Two sessions started and completed with `gpt-6.1-sol` / `openai` / medium,
without an independent provider receipt or installed-plugin trigger test.
Baseline and candidate reported 212,032 and 161,880 input tokens (including
175,616 and 138,752 cached), and 1,879 and 1,959 output tokens. The single
new synthetic case is not a general cost or quality estimate. Prompts,
commands, outputs, drafts and usage are retained in
`codex-relaxed-round-9-data.json`; hashes and dispatch counts are in the ledger.
