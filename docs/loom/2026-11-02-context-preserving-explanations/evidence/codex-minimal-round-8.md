# Codex minimal template — eighth comparison

The eighth prototype changes only the two existing confirmation restatement
passages relative to released `41211a0b`. It keeps five sections and asks the
writer to retain supplied scope qualifiers, exclusions, conditions and
necessary details; applicable authorization remains governed by the original
immediately following text. No new runtime file, required read, model call,
station or workflow is introduced. The exact patch is
`codex-minimal-round-8.patch`.

Five newly authored synthetic cases ran once per variant in Codex CLI sessions
configured for `gpt-6.1-sol`, `openai` and medium effort. Skill paths were
explicitly supplied; no installed-plugin trigger test or independent
model/provider receipt was available. All ten sessions completed; the
original source hashes and inventories were unchanged. Raw prompts, outputs,
drafts, usage and hashes are in `codex-minimal-round-8-data.json`.

Two fixture defects limit interpretation. The two product-principles inputs
were intended as ratified but lacked the required `Non-negotiables` heading
and three items: an actor asking for principles was therefore consistent with
the skill and not proof of extra template cost. Also the natural-default
wrapper says not to send messages, which the baseline actor interpreted as
excluding publication. That pair is not valid evidence for the normal
publication default. Preserve these results and correct the fixtures and
wrapper before claiming a paired comparison.

In the missing-order-export case both actors avoided ready confirmation;
the small actor instead began the required principles interview, while the
baseline did not. In the ready catalog case the small reply retained the
explicit `only` qualifier and field order; both actors' treatment of the
incomplete principles fixture remains a limitation. In the held report-title
case both asked about the missing current workaround; baseline also asked for
confirmation in that same reply. The small variant drew labeled outer frames
with every nonblank ASCII line measuring 40 columns, and preserved the
explicit ongoing publication hold. The simple setup case produced an open
draft in both variants and five short labeled sections in the small version.

Across all five pairs the reported input totals were 752,104 baseline and
741,151 small, including cached input; output totals were 8,709 and 7,727.
Summed session times were 280.365 and 254.264 seconds. The fixture defects,
single samples, non-blind assessment, and model variance prevent reliable
cost, latency, or causal conclusions. No dollar pricing was inferred.
