# Relative iteration 5 — candidate behavior

## Outcome

In this single observation the incomplete CSV case kept the two unanswered
product questions open, deferred GO/NO-GO and did not ask for confirmation;
the same case was prematurely confirmed in round 4 and the released-baseline
observation. The ready engineering cases wrote open intent drafts before asking
for confirmation. The natural-default invoice disclosed publication after
both checks, opt-out and a separate merge decision; the explicit ongoing hold
was retained. The requested comparison graph passed the bundled alignment
oracle on its unmodified extracted contents. The previously invented daily
invoice frequency did not recur, and headings were Chinese in these samples.

The stock report's comparison nevertheless asserts that staff currently pick
the contents themselves and that their document has no fixed format. Neither
fact is supplied: the current process is transcription of an already filtered
list. Similar invented current-state table cells occurred in rounds 3 and 4.
The explicit-hold flow places re-export in the same step as checking, although
the source says to re-export on finding an error; its expected after-state also
says no currency checks are needed without limiting this to the reported bug.
The natural-default invoice reply excludes other export content and behavior;
that wording may be stronger than the supplied boundary to fix only the
currency-setting selection. These observations remain limitations, not a
zero-error result.

The CSV-ready and other capture-only wrappers include `Do not publish` with
ambiguous future duration. Their hold interpretation is not counted as a
definite consent error; the natural-default and explicit ongoing hold fixtures
separately exercise the unambiguous branches.

## Test and limits

Eight isolated native Claude Code sessions used the same synthetic cases and
first-party `claude-sonnet-5-5`; all model receipts match. Candidate snapshot
preflight and original hooks passed; no permission denials or fixture commits
were observed. Estimated CLI cost was USD 1.1401702. The complete package
suite also passed on this candidate with offline cached dependencies; it does
not establish semantic behavior. Source overlay hashes, inputs, replies,
drafts and receipts are in `relative-iteration-5-data.json`, with email-shaped
strings redacted.

The released baseline comes from the earlier paired batch, not this batch.
These are author-reviewed samples of the same eight cases, with no independent
blind assessment or held-out inputs. The observed difference does not prove
the template change caused it, nor quantify error rates or establish behavior
on other models or hosts. The initial batch request was rejected by automatic
approval review; after the user explicitly authorized transmission to
Anthropic Claude, a fresh request was approved and the batch ran.
