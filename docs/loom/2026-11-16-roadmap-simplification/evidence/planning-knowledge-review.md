## Decision impact

The completed cases and separate clean acceptance witness support the requested
behavior under controlled source use. They do not show that the candidate is
better than the baseline, or that it eliminates execution friction. Criteria were
non-discriminating among completed outputs; do not keep tuning prompts or source
just to produce a favorable gain. Only the pre-existing 15pp threshold is waived.
The added prose makes the requested knowledge explicit without a new runtime,
schema, skill, compulsory checklist or cycle. Keeping the smaller old instruction
remains a viable alternative if codifying that guidance is not worth 26 lines.

This is not an installed-host trigger test, cross-platform study, genuine-user
behavior study or unconditional dogfood-pass. No source defect was confirmed by
the grader; the two first-attempt execution boundaries remain visible limitations.
Reviewers and the user's acceptance must see those limits rather than a green
mechanical suite being substituted for an unassisted semantic/product pass.
