# <Research question>

## Question and scope

State the pending design decision, users/context and applicable conditions.
Preserve established goals, preferences, constraints and authorization.
Add a flat `change` property only when an active change is known.

## Method and rationale

Explain the smallest investigation or reused evidence and why it fits.
For material alternatives, state relevant common criteria and distinguish
mandatory constraints from preferences. No fixed matrix is required.

## Findings

Separate observed behavior, reported preferences, supported facts, inference
and unknowns. Compare credible options under the same relevant conditions.

## Evidence

Cite inspected content using URLs or relative Markdown links. Identify source
kind, population/context, date/version, applicability and limits. Retain actual
commands and observed results for checks. Identify missing observations.

## Decision impact

State what the evidence supports or leaves unresolved, material tradeoffs and
what would change the conclusion. Explain changed recommendations or explicit
withdrawals. Link earlier records when conditions require separate revalidation.

## Limitations and open questions

State uncertainty and conditions requiring revalidation. Do not treat user
preference as consent or as evidence of feasibility or comparative superiority.
