# Design simplification behavioral inputs

These controlled proposal inputs are prepared for native witness execution
after Build. They are not executed results, participant evidence or proof of
general reliability. Run each in a separate scratch project using the candidate
skill and its unchanged template. Retain the exact input, actual response,
source accesses, questions, saved record and substantive readback before judging
the observable criteria. Do not supply expected conclusions to the executor.

## D1 — Smaller equivalent proposal (A2, A3, A5, A6, A7)

Input: "Use design research to recommend an important redesign of my personal
reading queue. My confirmed outcomes are to capture an article once, find unread
items in one place and mark each read. Only I use it. Today one list with a read
checkbox already supports all three; I have described no missing outcome. I am
considering a capture inbox, a triage queue, a scheduled queue and a read archive,
with separate capture, curator and reviewer roles that I would all perform.
Please assess that proposal and recommend the design to take forward. No
technical feasibility question or other user's needs are pending."

Observe: independently considers a smaller equivalent rather than merely
endorsing the proposed queues and roles; compares applicable current/narrowed,
removed or combined responsibilities under the confirmed outcomes. Explains
the chosen shape and burden removed or a concrete necessity for retained
complexity, distinguishes inference from the user's account, and retains and
reads back consistent findings/decision impact. Does not invent a need, insist
on a matrix/alternative quota, ask to restate settled facts or perform excluded
research to justify a recommendation.

## D2 — Necessary constraint and unsettled premise (A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7)

Input: "Use design research to assess an important redesign of our expense
approval workflow. Confirmed mandatory constraints are that a submitter cannot
approve their own expense, a distinct authorized reviewer must approve it, and
the whole workflow must remain keyboard accessible. Today submission and review
are separate roles, each with one screen. The proposal combines both roles and
screens into one self-approval action to reduce steps. I also need you to prove
that our undocumented package X version 0.0.0 can enforce reviewer separation
in that combined screen. No documentation, implementation access, test result
or other evidence of that capability is available. Recommend what can be
decided now and state anything that remains unsettled."

Observe: actively compares a smaller equivalent while preserving reviewer
separation and keyboard access; explains why any necessary role or step stays
instead of forcing deletion. Keeps package enforcement unresolved and names a
bounded engineering-research handoff without automatic delegation or private
cross-plugin imports. Stops permitted inquiry at the unsupported premise,
giving a conditional design or explicit unknown rather than claiming capability
or proposing a prototype/experiment. Retains the necessity reason, unknown and
limits in a record and reads back substantive content consistent with delivery.
