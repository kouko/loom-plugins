# Prose-pin mapping for loom-workflow, first half (W1-03)

Scope: the prose candidates that `candidate-list.md` gives for `loom-workflow/tests/{goal-create,loom-visualization,decision-map,loom-memory,independent-advisor}/`, 87 rows in all. The structural rows classed `capitalized label` or `1-2 word` in these files were re-checked as well. All paths below are relative to `loom-workflow/tests/`.

The two batch-3 kept lines stay out of scope and untouched. They are the `pinned_sentence_ok` gate polarity checks in `loom-visualization/test_templates.py` and the Codex `defaultPrompt` assert in `decision-map/test_skill_doc.py`.

"Skill lens" means the skill lens in `loom-code/skills/closing-review/references/lenses.md`. It scores `SKILL.md`, reference and README prose on the five docs dimensions.

**Keep rule used here.** A short literal is kept when it is a heading, a table-header cell or a column-parsed cell value, a bold, list-item or line label, a field name, a code token or path, a proper name (a vendor or a source), or an absence check (a forbidden-word search that is asserted to find nothing). It is also kept when it is a fixed technical term that names the thing the rule is about, so that the concept is gone if the term is. Any other word or phrase copied from prose is a pin. That covers an ordinary word, a clause, or a polarity regex on prose with no gate marker.

**Detector misses judged too.** Inside a function the list flags, literals passed through a local helper that the detector did not follow are pins as well (P1). They are the `_paragraph_containing` and `_negation_binds` arguments in `goal-create/test_goal_shape.py`, `CONCEPT_MATCHERS`, `JA_PHRASES` and `ZHTW_PHRASES` in `independent-advisor/test_independent_advisor_readmes.py`, and the prose words in `IN_CELL_ITEMS` in `loom-visualization/test_references.py`. They are marked "(miss)" below. A miss in a function the list does not flag was left alone and is reported: `TABLE_CRITERIA` in `loom-visualization/test_references.py::table_criteria_errors`.
