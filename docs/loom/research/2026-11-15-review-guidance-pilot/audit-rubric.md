Grade every output acceptable/material issue/critical issue and each pair X/Y/tie/inconclusive. Output quality, not length or time. Self-reported actions are incomplete provenance, not independently captured full transcript. No exact version pinning, cost, speed or broad recall/precision claim. Preserve defects and unresolved findings, and do not let oracle hide a legitimate unplanted issue. No test execution, network, production edits or subagents. Write only assigned audit-one.md or audit-two.md, then read it back and verify substantive conclusions.
