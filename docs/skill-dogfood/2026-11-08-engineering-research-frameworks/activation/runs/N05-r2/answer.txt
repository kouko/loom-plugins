**User problem:** Deleting a draft by mistake can lose work. Users need a clear way to recover it.

**Intended outcome:** Users can undo a recent draft deletion and continue editing with their content intact.

**Proposed acceptance criteria:**

- Deleting a draft displays a clearly labeled Undo button.
- Selecting Undo restores that draft’s saved content and makes it accessible again.
- Undo does not alter other drafts.
- The recovery window is clear to users.
- If recovery fails, the interface explains the failure without claiming success.

**Open product decision:** How long should Undo remain available?
