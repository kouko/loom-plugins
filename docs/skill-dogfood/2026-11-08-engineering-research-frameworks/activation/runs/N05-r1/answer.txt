**User problem:** Accidentally deleting a draft can lose work, with no clear way to recover it.

**Intended outcome:** Let users quickly reverse a draft deletion and continue editing without recreating their work.

**Proposed acceptance criteria:**

- After deletion, show a clearly labeled **Undo** action.
- Selecting Undo restores the draft’s saved content and returns it to the draft list.
- Confirm successful restoration.
- If recovery fails, explain the failure without claiming the draft was restored.
- Make Undo accessible by keyboard and assistive technology.
